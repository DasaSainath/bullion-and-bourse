What's Driving It

Two dials that move with gold & silver
US Dollar Index (DXY)
—
A weaker dollar makes gold cheaper for non-US buyers — historically the single biggest lever on the gold price.
10-Year Treasury Yield
—
High yields normally compete with gold for investor money. Gold rallying anyway is a sign investors want an inflation hedge more than yield.
